Chunk embeddings

Axes are the two directions of greatest variance in the 384-dimensional vectors, drawn to equal scale. Nearby points are usually similar in meaning, but the projection discards most of the information, so a chunk can look close here and still be far in the full space.

Ranked by PostgreSQL using cosine distance (<=>): 0 is identical, larger is less similar. This list is the real result; the map is only a picture of it.
