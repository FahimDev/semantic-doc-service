# Benefits and Leave

## Learning budget

Every full-time employee receives an annual learning budget of 1,200 USD for courses, books, and conferences. Manager approval is required before purchase, and reimbursement requests must include a receipt filed within 30 days.

## Annual leave

Employees accrue 1.75 vacation days per month, which is 21 days a year. Unused days up to five may roll over into the next calendar year. Submit leave requests at least two weeks ahead for absences longer than five days.

## Sick leave

Sick leave does not need advance approval. Notify your manager before your shift begins if you can. A medical certificate is required after three consecutive sick days so that payroll can record the absence correctly.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks. Leave may be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first twelve months after birth or adoption.

## Health insurance

Medical, dental, and vision coverage begins on your first day. The company pays 90 percent of the premium for employees and 70 percent for dependents. Enrollment changes are allowed during open enrollment each November or after a qualifying life event.

## Retirement plan

The company matches retirement contributions dollar for dollar up to 4 percent of salary. Matched funds vest gradually over three years, so leaving early forfeits part of the employer contribution.

## Wellness allowance

A monthly wellness allowance of 50 USD covers gym memberships, fitness classes, or meditation apps. Claim it through the expense portal with a receipt, and unused allowance does not carry over to the following month.

## Public holidays

The company observes twelve public holidays each year. When a holiday falls on a weekend, the nearest weekday becomes a paid day off. The holiday calendar is published every December for the coming year.
